StreakIO: Habit & Step Tracker

OFFICIAL EASODE APP

StreakIO is the flagship Android application designed, engineered, and published by Easode on Google Play. Built 100% in native Kotlin & Jetpack Compose with an offline-first architecture, it combines daily habit streaks, battery-saving background step counting during active hours, a calendar-based daily journal, milestone achievements, and offline data export in PDF or .bak backup format.
